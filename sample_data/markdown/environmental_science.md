# Environmental Science

## 1. Earth Systems

### Atmosphere
- **Composition**: N₂ (78%), O₂ (21%), Ar (0.9%), CO₂ (0.04%)
- **Layers**: Troposphere, Stratosphere, Mesosphere, Thermosphere
- **Greenhouse Effect**: CO₂, CH₄, H₂O trap heat

### Hydrosphere
- **Oceans**: 97% of Earth's water
- **Freshwater**: 2.5% (glaciers, groundwater, surface)
- **Water Cycle**: Evaporation → Condensation → Precipitation

### Biosphere
- All living organisms
- Ecosystems: Interactions between organisms and environment
- Biodiversity: Variety of life

## 2. Climate Change

### Greenhouse Gases
| Gas | Source | Global Warming Potential |
|-----|--------|--------------------------|
| CO₂ | Fossil fuels, deforestation | 1 (baseline) |
| CH₄ | Agriculture, landfills | 28-36 |
| N₂O | Agriculture, industry | 265-298 |
| F-gases | Industrial processes | 1,000-23,000 |

### Impacts
- Rising temperatures
- Sea level rise
- Extreme weather events
- Ocean acidification
- Biodiversity loss

### Mitigation
- Renewable energy
- Energy efficiency
- Carbon capture
- Reforestation
- Sustainable agriculture

## 3. Ecosystems

### Types
| Ecosystem | Location | Characteristics |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| Tropical Rainforest | Equator | High biodiversity, warm, wet |
| Desert | Various | Low precipitation, extreme temps |
| Tundra | Arctic/Alpine | Permafrost, low biodiversity |
| Coral Reef | Oceans | High biodiversity, sensitive |
| Grassland | Various | Moderate precipitation |

### Energy Flow
```
Sun → Producers (plants) → Primary consumers → Secondary consumers → Decomposers

10% Rule: Only ~10% of energy transfers between trophic levels
```

### Biogeochemical Cycles
- **Carbon Cycle**: Photosynthesis, respiration, decomposition
- **Nitrogen Cycle**: Fixation, nitrification, denitrification
- **Water Cycle**: Evaporation, condensation, precipitation
- **Phosphorus Cycle**: Weathering, erosion, sedimentation

## 4. Pollution

### Air Pollution
- **Sources**: Vehicles, industry, power plants
- **Pollutants**: PM2.5, O₃, NOₓ, SO₂, CO
- **Effects**: Smog, acid rain, health problems

### Water Pollution
- **Sources**: Industrial, agricultural, sewage
- **Pollutants**: Heavy metals, nutrients, pathogens
- **Effects**: Eutrophication, dead zones, health issues

### Soil Pollution
- **Sources**: Pesticides, industrial waste, mining
- **Pollutants**: Heavy metals, chemicals, plastics
- **Effects**: Reduced fertility, groundwater contamination
